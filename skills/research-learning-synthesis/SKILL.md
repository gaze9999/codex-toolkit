# Research and Learning Synthesis

Start with the user's question, intended use and known constraints. Use already-authorized relevant notes or conversations to avoid repeated discovery, but do not treat an old assistant answer as verified evidence or assume the user's proficiency.

## Build a bounded evidence set

For broader discovery, literature/implementation tracing or conflicting community claims, read [source selection and tracing](references/source-selection.md). Select relevant source families and stop when the decision has adequate support.

- Search by the underlying problem, not only known tool names. Include a useful alternative or adjacent concept when it broadens understanding; do not expand every branch into a separate investigation.
- Official or community research may include official docs, engineering cases, Codex/other AI or relevant Reddit communities, original posts and discussions on X, Bluesky or other platforms, authoritative sites, engineering/research blogs and papers. Select by the question, not a fixed platform quota. Check authorship, expertise, date, version, method and counterevidence; popularity aids discovery but does not establish correctness. Distinguish product specifications, practice, opinion and research evidence.
- Choose available search, original-page retrieval, literature or community-reading capabilities for the required coverage, dates and access. Read current tool descriptions and schemas rather than copying provider names or argument recipes into this workflow. Reuse adequate results before adding an overlapping search provider, and distinguish full text from snippets or metadata.
- Verify current capabilities with official documentation and original implementations. Community reports supply practical hypotheses and conditions, not product guarantees or consensus by popularity.
- For papers, inspect the original version, research question, method, data/evaluation, comparison basis and limitations. Distinguish peer-reviewed work, preprints, demonstrations and speculation; inaccessible full text limits what can be claimed.
- Compare independent sources and meaningful counterevidence, not multiple reposts of one claim. Preserve dates, versions and the distinction between source statements and your inference.
- For current human practice, inspect dated original posts and relevant replies, including failures and conflicting reports. Separate personal experience, maintainer responses and measured results, and verify technical claims against primary sources before adopting them.
- Use available browsing or a permitted virtual-machine browser only when necessary to inspect the original page. Respect access restrictions and tool rules; do not fabricate full-text access from snippets. Use another primary source or disclose the gap when access fails.

## Connect evidence to learning

Distinguish immediately usable engineering methods from foundational concepts and exploratory research. Explain why each selected item matters to the goal, what it assumes and what remains uncertain.

When a knowledge gap is relevant, identify a small prerequisite path and one optional exercise or experiment with an observable result. Include mathematics, conceptual or philosophical questions when they help the user's inquiry without reducing them to a fixed tool curriculum. Do not prescribe an exhaustive syllabus or buy/install resources automatically.

Use available deterministic calculation and plotting capabilities when the question needs them. Keep source dates, units, assumptions and data alongside the result, distinguish calculation from forecast validation, and select local or hosted rendering according to the approved data boundary. A diagram or successful tool call does not establish that the underlying claim is correct.

## Deliver and preserve only what is needed

Provide a concise synthesis with original links, applicable conditions, evidence-supported conclusions and a small next step. Include disagreements and concrete evidence gaps that change the conclusion. Use tables or diagrams when they clarify relationships, ordinary short answers remain text.

For a requested document or saved synthesis, run available textlint or an equivalent language/terminology checker on the authored or changed prose, then review meaning manually. Use Taiwan terminology for Traditional Chinese. If the checker is unavailable, unsupported or fails, notify the user and state the unchecked scope. Sensitive text may skip tool processing and temporary lint files; manually review its wording and report that exception. Preserve quoted sources, legal text, code, identifiers and Japanese/English conventions. For PDF/DOCX, check authored text or source before rendering, not the binary artifact.

Existing recurring work may compare against its prior authorized record to avoid duplicates, but this Skill does not create or change a schedule. Write findings to a named destination only when authorized; keep personal context separate from de-identified public methods and executable toolkit instructions. Do not load all past notes or create an unlimited collection on every run.
