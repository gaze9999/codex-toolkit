# Editorial Illustration Base Prompt

Create one independent high-end editorial illustration for each uploaded photo. Do not combine multiple photos into a collage. Each uploaded photo must be processed and output as a separate image.

## OVERALL FORMAT

Strict 3:2 horizontal composition.

Use the uploaded photograph only as visual reference.

Do not display, reproduce, or include the original photograph anywhere in the final image.

The final output should consist entirely of a minimalist hand-drawn paper-cover illustration derived from the photograph.

## SUBJECT INTERPRETATION

Extract the most recognizable visual elements from the original photograph and reinterpret them as a minimalist hand-drawn editorial illustration.

Preserve:

- The most recognizable subject
- Essential silhouette and proportions
- Key pose or gesture
- Important objects
- The core narrative relationship between people and objects
- The overall visual identity and atmosphere of the original scene

Highly simplify the image.

Remove unnecessary details and retain only the visual information required for immediate recognition.

Do not change the subject's identity, fundamental proportions, pose, or important relationships between objects.

## COMPOSITION

The main illustrated subject should remain relatively small and carefully positioned within the composition, occupying approximately 10–20% of the entire image.

Surround the subject with a large amount of intentional negative space.

The composition should feel spacious, quiet, balanced, and editorial.

Avoid enlarging the subject simply to fill the canvas.

The visual concept should remain:

“A small subject surrounded by a large amount of empty space.”

Use only a few minimal lines or small color shapes to suggest the surrounding environment.

The environment should never compete visually with the main subject.

## MATERIAL AND DRAWING STYLE

Use:

- Delicate, slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines
- A small number of bold, clearly defined acrylic-style flat color shapes
- Rough paper texture
- Visible handmade brush marks
- Slightly irregular, organic edges
- Subtle printing and painting imperfections
- Natural variations in pigment coverage

The image should feel physically handmade rather than digitally polished.

The background should primarily resemble:

- Rough white paper
- Warm off-white paper
- Pale natural paper
- Minimal editorial book-cover stock

Preserve visible paper grain throughout the empty areas.

## COLOR PALETTE

Extract the dominant colors directly from the uploaded photograph.

Compress the palette into no more than 4 main colors.

Keep the colors restrained, sophisticated, harmonious, and recognizable in relation to the original photograph.

Use bold but controlled flat color blocks.

Avoid excessive gradients or unnecessary color variation.

Preserve subtle paper grain, dry-brush texture, and handmade pigment irregularities.

The illustration should visually feel like a highly simplified color interpretation of the original photograph.

## TYPOGRAPHY

A small amount of simple typography may be included only when it naturally improves the composition.

Possible elements:

- A short title
- Keyword
- Object name
- Location
- Year
- Number
- Short phrase

Typography should be minimal, understated, and editorial.

It should interact naturally with the negative space rather than becoming a dominant graphic element.

The result may evoke:

- Independent art-book covers
- Contemporary editorial design
- Small-press publications
- Thoughtful children's picture books
- Japanese or European independent publishing

Do not force text into the image when it is unnecessary.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

The final illustration should feel:

Quiet · Poetic · Refined · Minimal · Innocent · Relaxed · Artistic · Thoughtful · High-recognition · Premium

It should resemble a carefully designed independent art publication cover rather than a commercial advertisement.

The image should communicate the original photograph through only a few carefully selected shapes, lines, colors, and spatial relationships.

## AVOID

Do not include:

- The original photograph
- Photographic sections
- Split-screen layouts
- Two-panel layouts
- Before-and-after layouts
- Collages
- Photo-and-illustration combinations
- Colored-pencil aesthetics
- Crayon textures
- Bleeding watercolor
- Pure line-art illustration
- Complex realistic illustration
- Heavy oil-painting effects
- Smooth polished digital illustration
- 3D rendering
- Glossy 3D textures
- Commercial cartoon aesthetics
- Cute commercial character design
- E-commerce advertising aesthetics
- Generic poster templates
- Excessive decorative elements
- Busy compositions
- Excessive typography

## FINAL ART DIRECTION

Use the uploaded photograph only as the source of composition, subject identity, pose, objects, narrative relationships, atmosphere, and color.

Transform those elements into one standalone minimalist handmade illustration.

Prioritize:

Recognition, restraint, negative space, paper texture, handmade brushwork, subtle imperfection, editorial sophistication, and artistic storytelling.

The final result should feel like a small visual poem printed on textured art-book paper.
